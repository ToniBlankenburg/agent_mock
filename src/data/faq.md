## Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Contact support to initiate.

## Refunds
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the return is received.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express is 1–2 days.

Support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 CET.
